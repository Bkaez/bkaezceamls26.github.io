what_i_learned: |
 Today, my team and I began writing the research paper for our research project, and I was assigned the responsibility of writing the introduction section. This task involved much more than simply introducing the topic. I had to provide background on PM₂.₅ pollution, explain why accurate air quality prediction is an important problem, establish the motivation for our research, define our research objectives, describe the novelty of our approach, and conclude the introduction by outlining the organization of the paper. 
 
 Throughout this process, I learned that a strong introduction plays a crucial role in setting the tone for the entire paper. It should gradually guide the reader from understanding the broader problem to appreciating the specific contribution of the research. I also gained a greater appreciation for the importance of supporting every major claim with credible sources, as this strengthens the paper's academic rigor and demonstrates how our work builds upon existing research. Overall, today's task reinforced that effective research is not only about developing a successful model but also about communicating its purpose, significance, and contributions in a clear and compelling manner.
