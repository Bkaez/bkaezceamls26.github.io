what_i_learned: |
 Today my team members and I focused on refining our research paper and our 90-second elevator pitch video. We continued making edits to the paper, improving the clarity, organization, and overall flow of our writing before submitting it. Although the revisions were relatively small, I learned that producing a strong technical paper requires multiple rounds of careful review and attention to detail.

We also finalized our elevator pitch video. To make the editing process more efficient, two of us independently created different versions of the video. After comparing both edits, we realized that each one had unique strengths and weaknesses. Rather than choosing a single version, we combined the best scenes, transitions, and editing choices from each to create a stronger final product. This process showed me that collaboration isn't always about everyone doing the same task, it can also involve combining different ideas to produce a better outcome.
