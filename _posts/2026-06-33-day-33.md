 Today, I worked on the conclusion section of our research paper, bringing together everything we've accomplished throughout the project into a concise summary. Writing the conclusion challenged me to think beyond individual experiments and focus on the bigger picture, what our research contributes, what the results mean, and why they matter.  In addition to the paper, I began drafting ideas for our 90-second project video. The biggest challenge was figuring out how to communicate several weeks of research in such a short amount of time. This required identifying the most important aspects of our project, its motivation, methodology, key results, and real-world impact, while keeping the message clear and engaging for a broad audience. It was a good exercise in simplifying technical concepts without losing their meaning.
