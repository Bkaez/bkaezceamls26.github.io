what_i_learned: |
 Today was mostly focused on improving the quality of our dataset and preparing it for better model performance. One of the tasks I worked on was fixing errors in my interpolated and resampled data. This involved checking whether the preprocessing steps had been applied correctly and making sure the data was organized in a way that would be useful for analysis and modeling.

 I also learned about hyperparameters and how they influence the behavior and performance of machine learning models. Unlike regular parameters that are learned from data, hyperparameters are settings chosen before training a model and can have a significant impact on the final results.
 
 Another major topic was feature engineering. I learned how creating new features can help a model make more accurate predictions. We experimented with lag features, which use previous PM2.5 values to help predict future values, rolling features that summarize trends over a period of time, and interaction features that combine information from multiple variables. It was interesting to see how modifying and creating features improved performance without changing the model itself.
