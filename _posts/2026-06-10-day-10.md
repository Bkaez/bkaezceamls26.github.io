what_i_learned: |
  Today was one of the more intense days in the research lab because we finally started working with the actual 
  datasets that we will be using throughout the research program. Most of the session with my graduate mentor 
  focused on data processing and preparing the datasets before analysis. We used Python in Google Colab and 
  applied a lot of the data handling techniques that we have been practicing over the past week using the pandas 
  library.
  
  One of the main tasks was individually merging four different datasets containing satellite AOD data from 
  different years into a single dataset. After that, we had to convert the AOD data from 10-minute intervals into 
  60-minute interval data so that it could better align with the meteorological datasets. We then merged the processed 
  AOD data with the weather data by matching the exact date and time values. Once everything was combined, we checked 
  the dataset for null values, missing values, negative values, and outliers, then applied different handling techniques 
  to clean the data.
  
  Today made me realize how much work goes into preparing data before you can actually analyze it. A lot of the process 
  involved cleaning, organizing, and making sure everything matched correctly across the datasets. Working with large 
  amounts of data was definitely challenging at times, but it gave me a better understanding of what real research work 
  looks like behind the scenes.
