 Today our team focused on creating and filming our 90-second elevator pitch, which challenged us to communicate our research in a clear and engaging way within a very short amount of time. I also helped direct parts of the video by applying techniques I've learned in the past, particularly the use of B-roll. Instead of relying only on someone speaking to the camera, B-roll footage helps visually reinforce what is being discussed by showing relevant clips of the research process and lab work. After filming, I spent time editing the video. It turned out to be a much more iterative process than I expected, requiring multiple rounds of trimming clips, adjusting timing, organizing transitions, and making sure the visuals matched the narration. Every small change improved the overall flow, showing me how much attention to detail goes into producing a polished final product. I lowkey think I shoulld venture into filmmaking lol.
