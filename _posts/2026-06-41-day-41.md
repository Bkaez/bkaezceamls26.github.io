 Today we spent most of our time working on the slides for our final symposium presentation. We've covered so much this summer, and one of our biggest tasks was figuring out how to present everything in a way that tells a clear and logical story. We worked on organizing our presentation from the motivation behind the project, to our methodology, and finally to the results and conclusions.

 We also spent time revisiting what makes our research unique. We focused on highlighting the different steps we took to improve our results, such as combining satellite-derived AOD data with meteorological data, performing data preprocessing, creating new features through feature engineering, and comparing both machine learning and deep learning models. Talking through these ideas helped me better understand that the novelty of a research project isn't always one big breakthrough, it can also come from thoughtfully combining different techniques to solve a problem more effectively.
