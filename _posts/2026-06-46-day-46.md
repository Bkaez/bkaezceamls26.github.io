  Today marked the second and final day of the symposium, bringing an end to an unforgettable summer research experience. My team had the opportunity to present our PM₂.₅ prediction research, sharing the work we've been developing over the past several weeks. Looking back at where we started, I was proud of how confidently we were able to explain our methodology, defend our decisions, and communicate our findings. It was also inspiring to watch the other research groups present. Every team approached a different problem, and seeing everyone's hard work come together made me appreciate how much we had all grown throughout the program.

  Another major highlight of the day was competing in my very first hackathon. My team developed Brain Builder, a gamified learning platform designed to encourage K–12 students to use AI as a learning companion rather than a shortcut. Instead of simply giving students answers, the platform challenges them to think critically, solve problems independently, and identify mistakes in AI-generated responses before receiving guidance. The goal is to promote ethical AI use while helping students develop stronger reasoning and problem-solving skills. Out of all the talented teams that competed, we were honored to receive second place. For my first hackathon, that was an incredible feeling and a memorable way to end the symposium.
