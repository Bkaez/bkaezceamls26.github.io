what_i_learned: |
 Today was the first day of the Summer AI Research Institute, and I honestly did not know 
what to expect. By the end of the day, though, it turned out to be a great learning experience 
filled with new ideas, technical skills, and opportunities to connect with others.
  
A special shout-out to Ms. Gabriella Waters for her presentation on Experimental Design.
She explained that experimental design is basically the “recipe” for research, which really 
helped me understand concepts like independent variables, dependent variables, control groups, 
and why having a clear hypothesis is important. One thing that stood out to me was her 
comparison of a good hypothesis to a GPS route because it tells you where you are going and 
how you will know when you have arrived.

We also discussed a study about generative AI in higher education and learned about tools and 
methods researchers use, such as descriptive statistics, and thematic analysis. It was 
interesting to see how research is organized and evaluated. Michael Adeleke also walked us 
through setting up GitHub and making changes to our webpages. That session gave me more confidence
in using GitHub and understanding how websites can be updated and managed.

Another fun part of the day was the virtual networking bingo game. It helped me interact with other 
participants and made me realize that I actually have a lot in common with many of my peers.Overall, 
the first day was both educational and enjoyable, and I am excited to learn even more throughout the 
rest of the program.
