what_i_learned: |
  I had a great time learning today because it was much more hands-on and interactive
  compared to a regular lecture. The sessions felt engaging, and I enjoyed being able 
  to actively practice what we were learning instead of only listening to presentations. 
  I also got the opportunity to meet my mentors and talk more about the project and 
  goals we will be working toward during the program.
  
  One of the highlights of the day was Elijah’s workshop on UI/UX design and Figma. He 
  explained the importance of user interface design, user experience, accessibility, and
  human-computer interaction in a way that was easy to understand. Since we had already 
  completed the UI/UX course on Codecademy beforehand many of the concepts connected 
  together much better and made more sense. The most exciting part for me was creating my 
  first-ever landing page. It was really cool seeing how layouts, colors, and design 
  principles can come together to create something visually appealing and functional.
  
  Later in the day, Dr. Mack taught us more about computer science fundamentals and 
  programming concepts. We discussed ideas such as computation, computational thinking, 
  algorithms, and coding. I learned that an algorithm is basically a set of steps used to 
  accomplish a task, while programming is writing those instructions for a computer to follow. 
  Dr. Mack also explained that computer science is everywhere in modern life, which made me 
  realize how connected technology is to almost everything we do today.
  
  We also began learning more about Python. I learned that Python is a programming language 
  designed for simplicity, making it beginner-friendly and easier to understand. Having some 
  basic programming experience beforehand really helped me follow along during the lesson. One 
  interesting thing I learned was that in Python, you can print numbers directly without assigning 
  them a data type first. Small concepts like that helped me better understand how programming 
  languages work behind the scenes.
