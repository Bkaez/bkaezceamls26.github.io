what_i_learned: |
 Today I learned some valuable insights in the workshop led by Dr. Shaun Gittens. The session focused on how artificial intelligence is evolving from traditional chatbots into AI agents that can plan, act, and interact with external tools and services. I learned that while chatbots are mainly designed for conversation, AI agents can perform tasks, access APIs, and coordinate actions to achieve specific goals.

 Another key takeaway was the shift in software development from writing code line by line toward defining goals, constraints, and desired outcomes. As AI tools become more capable, the bottleneck is moving away from syntax and toward problem formulation, decision-making, and evaluation. I also learned about concepts such as agent teams, subagents, and the importance of human oversight in AI-driven workflows.
